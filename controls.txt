===============================================================================
  THE FREAK  //  DYNAMIC EQ  //  Castle Danger Music Company
===============================================================================

The Freak is an EQ whose bands move by themselves. Each of its six bands has a
normal static GAIN, plus a DEPTH that lets it react when its own frequency range
misbehaves:

  - Negative DEPTH: the band CUTS when that range jumps more than THRESHOLD dB
    above its normal level (boomy notes, harsh peaks, cymbal crashes).
  - Positive DEPTH: the band LIFTS when that range sinks more than THRESHOLD dB
    below its normal level (a mix that goes dull in quiet parts).

"Normal" is the band's own average over the last few seconds, learned while the
band has something in it. So a band set on a quiet verse still behaves in the
loud chorus, and silence never makes it lift. Zero latency, stereo linked.

Put it first on the master bus (The Freak > Chop Shop > Dominatrix > The Master),
or on any track or bus.


THE CURVE
---------
  Faint hill ............ the output spectrum
  Grey line ............. the static EQ (GAIN only)
  Bright line ........... the live EQ, including what the bands are doing now
  Coloured area ......... how far the selected band can move (its DEPTH)
  Tether under a node ... the band has moved from its resting spot

  Drag a node ............ frequency (left/right) and gain (up/down)
  Cmd-drag / Alt-drag .... DEPTH (up/down)
  Scroll over a node ..... width (Q)
  Double-click a node .... switch the band on or off
  Dragging a band that's off switches it on.


BANDS
-----
Click a row to select a band, double-click to switch it on or off. The little
bar on the right shows the live move: left = cutting, right = lifting.


BAND CONTROLS (selected band)
-----------------------------
  ON ............ band on/off
  BELL / LOW SHELF / HIGH SHELF
                  A low shelf moves everything below FREQ and listens to the
                  lows; a high shelf moves everything above and listens to
                  the highs.
  LISTEN ........ hear only what this band is listening to, to find the
                  problem. Turn it off before you bounce. (Never saved in a
                  preset.)
  FREQ .......... 20 Hz - 20 kHz
  WIDTH (Q) ..... 0.3 - 8. Higher = narrower. For a shelf it sets the slope.
  GAIN .......... -12 to +12 dB, always applied
  DEPTH ......... -18 to +12 dB, the most the band may move on its own.
                  0 = a plain static EQ band.
  THRESHOLD ..... 0 - 18 dB: how far the range must stray from normal before
                  the band moves. The cut is 3/4 of a dB for every dB over the
                  threshold, up to DEPTH; the lift is 1/2 dB per dB under.

The yellow line under the knobs is The Master's reason for the band, when The
Master set it.


GLOBAL
------
  AMOUNT ........ scales every band's DEPTH. 0% = static EQ only, 200% = double.
  SPEED ......... how fast bands react and let go (slow ~30 ms / 400 ms,
                  fast ~3 ms / 60 ms)
  OUTPUT ........ -12 to +12 dB
  IN / OUT ...... peak meters


THE EYE
-------
It watches. The harder the bands work, the wider and redder it gets.


WITH THE MASTER
---------------
The Freak talks to The Master over Castle Danger Link. When The Master analyses
your mix it looks at how much each frequency range swings between the quiet and
loud parts. At PRESCRIBE it sets The Freak's bands on the ranges that swing too
much (or go missing), with a short reason for each, and turns off its own
built-in tone EQ so the two don't fight. LINKED TO THE MASTER lights up when it
can see The Master; SET BY THE MASTER flashes when it was just set.


PRESETS
-------
Factory presets cover the master bus, low end, mud, harshness, cymbals, kick &
bass, vocals and guitars. SAVE stores your own; they live in
  macOS:   ~/Library/Application Support/Castle Danger/The Freak/Presets
  Windows: %APPDATA%\Castle Danger\The Freak\Presets

REBEL  (next to the link lamp)
  On: The Master keeps its hands off this plugin. Nothing it sends changes a setting here; it still
  hears what the plugin does and plans the rest of the mix around it. Saved with the project; presets
  never change it. Turn it off to let The Master run the plugin again.
